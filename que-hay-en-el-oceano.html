¿Qué hay en el océano?

Ocean animals and hay (there is / there are)

Teacher tip: before students tap, ask “¿Qué hay en el océano?” and have them point or answer in the chat. After each tap, everyone reads the Hay… sentence out loud.

Teacher tip: students tell you A, B, or C (or type it in the chat). After the right answer, everyone says the sentence out loud.
